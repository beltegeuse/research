### Theme

The theme for the 2025 rendering competition was "The Evolution of Time". The entries were received and judged on both technical complexity and artistic merit by Adrien Gruson and two external judges.

### External judges

- [Olivier Therrien](https://www.linkedin.com/in/olivier-therrien-90200836/?originalSubdomain=ca) (CDRIN)
- [Damien Rioux-Lavoie](https://riouxld21.github.io/research/) (Ubisoft - Laforge)
